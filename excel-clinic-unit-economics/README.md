# Clinic Growth & Unit Economics (Excel)

Excel analysis of a medical clinic's client base and marketing, with a unit-economics model and a forecast.

**Sheets:** Report (cohorts and retention by period) · Dashboard Presentation · Branding (acquisition sources) · Needs (client profiles) · Requests · Visits (doctors, services, cost and price, feedback) · Pivot tables · Hypotheses

- Client acquisition and retention by period: new and returning clients
- Unit economics: ACV, costs, visits and cumulative profit by period, with a scenario of +10% ACV
- Doctor performance: revenue, material costs, service quality from feedback
- Advertising vs referral acquisition and its link to NPS

The dataset is a synthetic training case. Names are generated.
