01 · CRM Data Cleaning and Preparation¶

Project: CRM analysis of online programming school X (July 2023 – June 2024). Goal of this notebook: turn 4 raw CRM exports into clean, linked tables that support funnel, marketing-efficiency and unit-economics analysis.

Table Contents Key
Contacts contacts (leads) Id
Calls sales managers' calls CONTACTID → Contacts
Spend daily ad spend Source, Campaign → Deals
Deals potential deals Contact Name → Contacts

All cleaning logic lives in the module src/cleaning.py so it can be reused and tested. This notebook checks the raw data, runs the ETL and validates the result.

def missing_report(df):
    m = df.isna().sum()
    return (pd.DataFrame({"missing": m, "%": (m / len(df) * 100).round(1),
                          "dtype": df.dtypes.astype(str)})
            .query("missing > 0").sort_values("%", ascending=False))

2. Cleaning rules and why¶

# Table Problem Fix Reason
1 Deals Lost Reason = Duplicate drop per the FAQ this is a technical duplicate lead; keeping it inflates leads and deflates conversion
2 Deals Closing Date < Created Time; Closing Date after the export date (21.06.2024) → NaT a deal cannot close before it is created (FAQ: data error, the analyst decides); a future date is a plan, not a fact
3 Deals Initial Amount Paid > Offer Total Amount 2 rows with offer 1,200 on an 11-month course are a typo in the offer; elsewhere initial is capped at offer the first payment cannot exceed the course price; conservative revenue estimate
4 Deals offer = €0 / 1 / 9 flag is_demo per the FAQ these are demo accesses for a token fee, not students
5 Deals Payment Done but Months of study empty not counted as a student FAQ: "if the month is not filled in, the student is not studying and the money must not be counted"
6 Deals amounts as text, SLA of mixed type → float / seconds calculations need numbers
7 Deals Level of Deutsch as free text (Cyrillic б1, в1, "B1 (until February)") → CEFR A0…C2 Cyrillic б/в = B; take the current level (the first one mentioned)
8 Deals cities: -, street addresses, different spellings → city, country needed for geographic analysis
9 Deals Content, Term drop ad-level detail; campaign and source are kept
10 Deals categorical gaps explicit labels Unknown, Not rated, Not lost the gap itself is informative; dropping rows would distort the funnel
11 Calls Dialled Number, Tag 100% empty drop no information
12 Calls no duration drop rows (83) a call without a duration cannot be analysed
13 Calls Outgoing Call Status empty for inbound calls → Not applicable not missing, just not applicable
14 Spend 917 full duplicates drop all are zero rows and do not affect totals
15 Spend Campaign empty → (no campaign) the spend is real and must not be dropped

Cash and revenue. Per the FAQ, payment is confirmed by the status Payment Done, and the first payment is Initial Amount Paid. The rest of the course is paid monthly, so the cash received by the export date is:

$$cash\_received = initial + (months\_of\_study - 1) \cdot \frac{offer - initial}{course\_duration - 1},\quad \le offer$$

For a one-off payment (initial = offer), the whole offer is received. By law, however, revenue is recognised only for the months studied, so the analysis uses

$$revenue = \frac{offer}{course\_duration} \cdot months\_of\_study,\quad prepaid = \max(cash\_received - revenue, 0)$$

prepaid is an advance for future months; for students who dropped out it is an amount to refund (refund_due), see notebook 03.

3.1 Univariate analysis again for the columns where missing values were filled¶

Missing values in categorical fields were filled with explicit labels (Unknown, Not rated, Not lost, (no campaign), Not applicable). We check what the distributions look like afterwards: what share the label takes, which value became the mode, and whether the distribution of the known values changed.

FILLED = [("Deals", "quality", "Not rated"), ("Deals", "lost_reason", "Not lost"),
          ("Deals", "campaign", "(no campaign)"), ("Deals", "product", "Unknown"),
          ("Deals", "education_type", "Unknown"), ("Deals", "payment_type", "Unknown"),
          ("Deals", "city", "Unknown"), ("Deals", "german_level", "Unknown"),
          ("Calls", "outgoing_call_status", "Not applicable"), ("Spend", "campaign", "(no campaign)")]
rows = {}
for table, col, label in FILLED:
    s = clean[table][col].astype(str)
    known = s[s.ne(label)]
    rows[f"{table}.{col}"] = {"label": label, "label share": s.eq(label).mean(), "unique": s.nunique(),
                              "mode": s.mode().iloc[0], "mode of known values": known.mode().iloc[0],
                              "share of that mode among known": known.value_counts(normalize=True).iloc[0]}
univariate = pd.DataFrame(rows).T
univariate.style.format({"label share": "{:.1%}", "share of that mode among known": "{:.1%}"})

Findings of the repeated univariate analysis:

  • In quality and lost_reason the label is not the mode: 11% of leads are not rated and 28% are "not lost" (the deal is still open or won). The distribution of the known values did not change; filling only made the gap visible.
  • In product, education_type, payment_type, city and german_level the Unknown label takes 82–98% and becomes the mode. This is expected: these fields are filled in at the consultation. So in the EDA these fields are analysed on known values only, and the share of Unknown is itself a CRM data-quality metric (notebook 04, action plan).
  • In Calls.outgoing_call_status, 9% "Not applicable" are incoming calls, not missing data.
  • Filling with the mean or the mode would distort the picture: the mode of product would turn 82% of leads into "Digital Marketing", and conversion by product would rise several times.

4. Summary¶

  • Deals after cleaning: 19,822 (1,771 technical duplicates removed), calls: 95,791, spend rows: 19,862.
  • Students (paid + study months filled in, not demo): 837 of 856 Payment Done deals.
  • The joins work: 99.8% of deals and 100% of calls with a contact find their contact.
  • Clean tables are saved to data/processed/ in two formats: CSV (for Tableau/Sheets) and pickle (for Python: keeps the datetime, category and bool types and loads instantly).
